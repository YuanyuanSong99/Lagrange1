Hey there! I'm a postdoc at MIT, working with Dr. Gaël Forget in Prof. Mick Follows group. My research interests lie broadly in interaction between pole regions (Arctic and Antarctica) and mid-latitude climate, ocean heat transport and storage under climate change, as well as long-term trend and decadal varibility in atmosphere and ocean.

The key scientific questions to me are:

  - Where does the extra heat trapped in Earth go?

  - How does the extra heat influence our living enviroment?

  - What actions can we take to save ourselves from climate crisis?

Keeping these questions in mind, I wanna explore these research directions in the future:
  - Climate impacts of aerosols
  - Carbon dioxide storage in the ocean
  - Wild fires
  - Biological behaviours
  - Machine learning
  - Other multidisiplinary directions

My MBTI is ENTJ and I'm a sagittarius. I'm open to any interesting and potential multidisiplinary collaborations. Feel free to reach out!
