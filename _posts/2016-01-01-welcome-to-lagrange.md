I'm a Postdoc at MIT, working with Dr. Gaël Forget in Mick Follows group. My research interests lie broadly in interaction between pole regions (Arctic and Antarctica) and mid-latitude climate, ocean heat transport and storage under climate change, as well as long-term trend and decadal varibility in atmosphere and ocean.
